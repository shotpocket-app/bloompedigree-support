BloomPedigree

Keep your flower crosses, seedling observations and photo pedigrees together, season after season.

BloomPedigree is an independent iPhone journal for flower hybridizing. It is in development and is not yet available.

What it records

  • Parent plants and crosses: the seed parent and a pollen parent, Self or Unknown.
  • Seed batches and what you observe: pollinated flowers, pods, harvested seeds, sown seeds, germinated seedlings, flowering seedlings and retained seedlings.
  • Seasonal observations for each plant, kept separate for different years.
  • Photos, stored in the app as optimized copies, and pedigrees that display the parent relationships you recorded.
  • A review reminder for a cross, at a date and time you choose.

English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese are supported.

Free features and BloomPedigree Pro

The free allowance is five active, non-sample crosses. Plant records, completed and archived crosses have no set count limit. You can keep viewing and editing existing records, adding photos and observations, adding offspring to existing crosses, viewing pedigrees, exporting CSV, and creating or restoring complete photo backups without subscribing.

BloomPedigree Pro is a monthly, automatically renewing subscription. It adds unlimited active crosses and creation of formatted photo pedigree images and PDFs. Eligible customers can start a 3-day free trial; Apple determines eligibility. After the trial, the subscription renews at the monthly App Store price unless cancelled. The purchase screen shows the current price, billing period and applicable offer before you subscribe.

If Pro ends, your records remain accessible and editable, including active crosses already above the free allowance. Backups and restores never require Pro.

Your garden, your backups

BloomPedigree processes and stores your garden on your iPhone. This version has no BloomPedigree account, developer-operated garden server or automatic garden synchronization.

Important

The app does not provide automatic garden backup or synchronization. You are responsible for keeping your backup files. Restoring a backup replaces the entire current garden; it does not merge gardens.
