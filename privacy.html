BloomPedigree Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

BloomPedigree is an independent iPhone journal for flower hybridizing. It is developed and supported by the developer who uses the GitHub account shotpocket-app. This policy describes the app's local data handling, optional support correspondence and this support website. Contact: shotpocket6@gmail.com.

Garden records and photos

You choose the content you record: plant names and identifiers, parent relationships, crosses, seed batches, dates, counts, seasonal traits, selection decisions, notes and photos. BloomPedigree processes and stores this garden on your iPhone. This version has no BloomPedigree account, developer-operated garden server or automatic garden synchronization.

The system photo picker gives the app the images you select; it may retrieve a selected image from your photo provider, including iCloud. Apple's services and the provider's practices apply to that retrieval. The camera is used only after you choose to take a photo and grant permission. Selected or captured images are re-encoded into optimized copies for app storage; original photograph files are not the app's archival backup.

Length-unit preferences are stored locally. The app uses the system language and region to present its interface and measurements. Review reminders use Apple's local notification system. A notification can contain a cross identifier; you can control notification previews and permission in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and purchase restoration. BloomPedigree uses StoreKit product information and verified transaction, expiry and revocation information to show the offer and determine Pro access. We do not receive payment-card details through the app. Product, purchase and restore requests may contact Apple's services under Apple's privacy policy.

Backups, exports and deletion

The app creates complete backups, CSV record exports and eligible pedigree image/PDF exports on the device. You choose where to save or share them. A chosen file provider or recipient may store or transmit its copy under its own practices. Full backups contain your recorded garden and the app's photo copies and are not encrypted by BloomPedigree, so keep them in a location you trust. CSV includes record details and notes. Pedigree exports include selected record details; PDF metadata includes BloomPedigree as creator and a title containing the plant identifier.

Local garden data stays in app storage unless replaced or removed. Restoring a backup replaces the current garden rather than merging it. Removing the app's local data can remove saved records and photos. BloomPedigree has no remote garden recovery or deletion service.

Deleting or replacing local content does not delete your original photo-library files, previous exports, backups or recipient copies. You manage those separately. Apple's device backup services may include app data according to your settings. BloomPedigree itself does not make automatic garden backups or synchronize gardens. Restoring a purchase restores eligible access, not deleted records.

Advertising and analytics

This version includes no advertising, tracking SDK, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated analytics service. Apple may process platform diagnostics or TestFlight feedback according to your device, account and testing settings. These platform services are separate from optional support messages you send to the developer.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. They are used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by the providers involved, including Gmail for the support mailbox.

Correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or as required by law. To request access, correction or deletion of correspondence you have sent, contact shotpocket6@gmail.com with “BloomPedigree privacy” in the subject. This does not give the developer access to your local garden.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary private content. A backup can contain the entire garden; send one only when you understand and accept what it contains.

This support website

When these support pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits without signing in. BloomPedigree has added no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, cookies or local-storage tracking to these pages. Visiting the website does not upload your garden records or photos from the app. GitHub handles website visitor information under its Privacy Statement. Email and other external links open the service you choose, whose own practices apply.

Policy updates and contact

This policy will be updated if the app or support practices change. Contact shotpocket6@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

BloomPedigree is an independent iPhone journal for flower hybridizing. It is in development and is not yet available.
