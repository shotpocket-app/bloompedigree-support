Start with the sample garden

Open Settings and choose Load sample garden. Open Crosses and select 24-014 to explore its sowing batch and seedlings. Open seedling 24-014-A to see seasonal observations and its pedigree.

Sample records are marked Sample and do not use your free active-cross allowance. The sample flower pictures are AI-generated illustrations, not photographs of actual named cultivars or real breeding results. Removing the sample garden also removes observations and photos you added to those sample records. Back up first if you want to keep them. Removal is refused when your own records still reference sample records; resolve those links first.

Record your own cross

Add your parent plants with Add plant. In Crosses, add a cross, choose the seed parent and either a pollen parent, Self or Unknown, enter the date and number of flowers pollinated, and save. When both parents are specified, the swap control helps record a reciprocal cross.

Record the seed batch and update what you observe: pollinated flowers, pods, harvested seeds, sown seeds, germinated seedlings, flowering seedlings and retained seedlings. Germination rate uses seeds sown, not seeds harvested. The stages use different units; they are not all percentages of the preceding stage.

Open a plant and use Add observation for each season. Keep observations for different years separate. Choose traits in Settings → Trait templates. Flowering and selection decisions remain your observations; the app does not infer a breeding result from a photograph.

Units, language and reminders

Settings → Length units follows the iPhone region by default, with a manual choice of centimetres or inches. Changing the display unit does not rewrite recorded measurements.

Use Settings → Open iPhone Settings to change the app language. English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese are supported.

A cross can have a review reminder for a date and time you choose. Notification permission is requested when you first enable a reminder. iPhone notification and Focus settings can prevent delivery; check the cross again if its reminder says it is not scheduled. When there are many reminders, the nearest ones are scheduled first; open the app periodically so later reminders can be scheduled. A reminder is a prompt to inspect the plant, not a prediction that a pod will have formed. Cross codes may appear in notification previews.

Free features and BloomPedigree Pro

The free allowance is five active, non-sample crosses. Plant records, completed and archived crosses have no set count limit. You can keep viewing and editing existing records, adding photos and observations, adding offspring to existing crosses, viewing pedigrees, exporting CSV, and creating or restoring complete photo backups without subscribing.

BloomPedigree Pro is a monthly, automatically renewing subscription. It adds unlimited active crosses and creation of formatted photo pedigree images and PDFs. The purchase screen shows the current App Store price and billing period before you subscribe.

If Pro ends, your records remain accessible and editable, including active crosses already above the free allowance. Creating or reactivating another active cross requires an available free slot or Pro. You can mark a finished cross completed or archived to free a slot. Existing exported files remain yours to keep. Backups and restores never require Pro.

Manage or restore a subscription

Open Settings → View BloomPedigree Pro for the offer. Restore Purchases is available in Settings and on the Pro screen. Use the Apple Account that bought the subscription; a connection to Apple's services may be needed. Manage subscription in Settings opens Apple's subscription management.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

A cancelled, pending or unverified purchase is not a confirmed Pro unlock. If price or access cannot be confirmed, try again when connected. Your free records and backup tools remain available. Restoring purchases restores eligible Pro access; it does not recover a lost garden.

Back up before changing phones

  1. Open Settings → Create backup.
  2. Wait for preparation, tap Save backup…, then save through the system file screen. A prepared file has not yet been saved to your chosen destination.
  3. Choose a place separate from this app's local storage, such as your own cloud drive or a computer. Check that the saved file is available there.
  4. Keep dated copies, especially before replacing a garden or changing phones.

Important

A complete backup contains records, relationships and the photo copies stored by the app. Backup files are not encrypted by BloomPedigree; choose a trusted storage location. The app does not provide automatic garden backup or synchronization. You are responsible for keeping your backup files. iPhone backup services have their own settings and do not replace this workflow.

Important

To recover a saved garden, choose Settings → Restore from backup. Select a BloomPedigree backup and let the app check it. Restore and replace replaces the entire current garden; it does not merge gardens. Newer records absent from that backup will no longer be shown, and this version has no undo for replacement. Back up the current garden first if you want to keep it. Cancel before confirming if it is the wrong file.

Important

If validation fails, do not delete the current app data. Keep the original file and the error message. A newer backup format may require a newer app version. Damaged or incomplete files cannot be assumed recoverable.

After a successful restore, reminders are reconciled with the restored garden. Enabled future reminders require system permission; past reminders are not replayed. Restoring does not itself request notification permission.

CSV is for records, not restoration

Settings → Export records as CSV → Save files… creates nine data tables and a README for spreadsheets or other tools. It contains your record details and notes, including photo details but not the photo files. Table headings and stable status identifiers use English, and timestamps use UTC. It cannot be imported to restore a garden in this version. Use a complete backup for restoration. Keep the ten exported files together.

Photos and pedigrees

Choose a photo with Apple's system photo picker, or take one with the camera when available. Broad photo-library access is not required by the picker. Camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a photo.

The app stores optimized image copies. Keep original photographs separately when you need their original size or quality. A photo missing from storage cannot be recreated from its thumbnail as an original. Preserve your data and check a complete backup before removing the app during troubleshooting.

Open a plant's View pedigree → Export pedigree (shown as Export pedigree (Pro) without Pro). Choose PNG image or PDF document, then Create file → Share file. Export includes the whole pedigree for the selected generation depth, not only the area currently visible after zooming; the PDF is one page.

Pedigrees display the parent relationships you recorded. Unknown parents remain unknown. Viewing the pedigree is free; creating its formatted image or PDF requires Pro. Share only photos and record details you have permission to share.

Contact

Send a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing it; remove private details. Do not send a full garden backup unless needed for the issue and you are comfortable sharing its contents. Never send passwords or payment-card details.
