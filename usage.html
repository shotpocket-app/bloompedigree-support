Using BloomPedigree

BloomPedigree is independent of all plant societies and cultivar registration organizations. It does not register a cultivar or certify a pedigree. Its diagrams and statistics summarize the records you enter; they do not establish genetic parentage or guarantee pollination, germination, flowering or breeding results.

Use a physical label as well as the journal where practical, and check plant identifiers and both parents when recording a cross. Unknown parentage should remain unknown rather than being guessed. A review reminder uses the time you choose; there is no universal three-day pod-setting rule across the plants this app can record.

Record traits for the relevant growing season. Plants and conditions vary; preset traits and scores are organizational tools, not universal breeding standards. Display-unit changes do not change the underlying measurement.

Keep regular complete backups somewhere separate from this app. You are responsible for keeping your backup files. CSV exports and pedigree images/PDFs do not replace a restorable photo backup. Restoring replaces the current garden without merging, so back up newer work before confirming a replacement. Keep original photos separately if you need archival originals.

The built-in sample is fictional. Its AI-generated flower illustrations show how photo records and pedigrees work; they do not document named cultivars or real breeding outcomes. Only add or share content you have permission to use.

The software's Terms of Use are the Apple Standard EULA. Nothing in this usage guide removes rights provided by applicable law.

BloomPedigree is an independent iPhone journal for flower hybridizing. It is in development and is not yet available.
